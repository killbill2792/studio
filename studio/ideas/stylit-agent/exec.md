# Exec: Stylit Agent

Status: proposed; owner starts work by merging this PR.

## Goal
Stylit is an AI fashion agent. For Gen Z, style is identity: show who someone could be, and act before they ask. Replace Shop with one Stylist screen. All app code stays in killbill2792/MVPStyli.

## Why this matters
Make looks, try-ons, products, Pods and advice one visual conversation. Pods remain the proof from real people, not just an AI opinion.

## Success looks like
The screen leads with an outfit image; suggestions change that image instead of producing long text. It offers three closet-based looks, proactive Pods, a nightly tomorrow look, and sized store links. Existing features still work.

## Out of scope
